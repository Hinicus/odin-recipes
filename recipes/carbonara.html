Creamy Carbonara

This is carbonara but is very creamy. It's delicious, quick, easy, no stress and basically foolproof

Ingredients

  • Parmesan
  • Spaghetti
  • Eggs
  • Basil
  • Pancetta (or Guanchiale or Bacon)
  • Half and Half

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta: cook it until al dente. Drain but do oot rinse.
  2. Crisp the panetta (or whatever you used): Skillet over medium-low heat, saute until golden and crispy.
  3. Build the Sauce: add the half and half to skillet and bring it to a gentle simmer.
  4. Bring it all together: toss in the drained pasta, the crispy pancetta, and that chopped fresh basil.

Pad Thai Recipe

Pad Thai is one of the world’s most beloved noodle dishes. Along with Thai Green Curry and Red Curry, this is the dish by which every Thai restaurant is measured.

Pad Thai is a Thai noodle stir fry with a sweet-savoury-sour sauce scattered with crushed peanuts. It’s made with thin, flat rice noodles, and almost always has bean sprouts, garlic chives, scrambled egg, firm tofu and a protein – the most popular being chicken or prawns/shrimp.

Ingredients

  • Pad Thai Sauce (Tamarind Puree or Ketchup)
  • Rice Noodles (preferrably Chang's “Thai Style” rice noodles)
  • Tofu
  • Garlic Chives
  • Chicken

Steps

  • Boil the rice noodles
  • Create the pad thai sauce with the tamarind or ketchup
  • Stir fry the chicken, and tofu
  • Add the rice noodles and stir
  • Lastly, add the garlic chives
  • Serve and ready to eat!
